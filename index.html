About Me

Hi! I'm a PhD at The Ohio State University (Go Bucks!), advised by Prof. Srinivasan Parthasarathy. I am interested in all things related to graphs including graph neural networks, graph mining, and network science. Additionally, I do research in conformal prediction and fairness.

In addition to doing research, I am a teaching associate for the Fundamentals of Engineering for Honors program.

Previously, I have received a Bachelor's and Master's degree in Computer Science & Engineering and Mathematics at The Ohio State University.
